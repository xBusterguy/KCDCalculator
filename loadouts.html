Loadout Builder

Pick a full set of armour and a weapon for each loadout, set a crafting level, and see what it costs to craft. Copy it as text or save it as a picture to share. The fill buttons prefer armour pieces and use clothing only where a slot has no armour. Material prices are shared with the crafting calculator. Use “Copy link” to share a loadout; the link carries the pieces but not your prices.
