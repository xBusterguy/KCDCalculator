KCD Tools

Calculators for Kingdom Come: Deliverance II roleplay. Enter your own prices, pick a craft or crop, and see what is worth your time.

Crafting cost calculator →

Material prices, bench fees and markup for Smithing, Armouring, Tailoring, Bowyery, Jewelling, Alchemy, Brewing, Cooking and Carpentry. Build an order and get a shopping list.

Farming efficiency →

Compare every crop by value per harvest, value per hour and value in a time window, for your trade level and strip conditions.

Loadout builder →

Set up armour and weapon loadouts at different levels and see what each costs to craft. Copy a loadout as text or save it as a picture.

Good to know

Everything you enter stays in your own browser, so nothing is shared between devices.
